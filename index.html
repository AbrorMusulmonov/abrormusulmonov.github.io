Abror Musulmonov

Hi, I’m Abror. I’m an AI researcher from Uzbekistan, interested in medical AI and natural language processing for languages with limited data.

At EPFL’s LiGHT Laboratory, I work on multilingual medical AI through the Meditron project. Previously, I was a research assistant at the University of Münster’s Sunrise AI Future Hub. My background is in computer and software engineering at Inha University in Tashkent.

I’ve also worked on Turkic language datasets at Tilmoch and co-founded PureTalent, a platform for educational mentorship.
